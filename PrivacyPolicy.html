Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026 (v1.7.7)

CopyMyCart respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension handles your information.

Information Collection

CopyMyCart is designed with a privacy-first architecture. I do not collect, store, transmit, or harvest any personal data, user credentials, browsing history, or personal identifiers.

Local Storage

All cart items, folders, and preferences you save using CopyMyCart are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (browser.storage.local). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to me or any third parties.

Permissions Usage

To provide its core functionality, CopyMyCart requests specific browser permissions, which are strictly limited to local execution:

  • activeTab & Host Permissions: Interacts exclusively with active Amazon and eBay cart pages to read product data only when you explicitly trigger the extension.
  • scripting: Executes local scripts to parse cart contents directly within your browser.
  • storage: Manages your saved custom cart folders locally on your machine.
